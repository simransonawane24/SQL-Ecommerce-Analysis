# Business Insights

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify revenue drivers, profitable categories, regional performance, and the impact of discounting on profitability.

---

## KPI Summary

- Total Sales: 2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: 286,397.02
- Total Quantity Sold: 37,873

---

## Regional Analysis

- West region generated the highest sales revenue (725,457.82).
- West region generated the highest profit (108,418.45).
- West region achieved the highest profit margin (14.94%).
- Central region recorded the lowest profit margin (7.92%).
- South region generated the lowest sales revenue.

---

## Category Analysis

- Technology is the highest-performing category in both sales and profit.
- Office Supplies generated the highest quantity sold.
- Furniture generated high revenue but relatively low profit.

---

## Sub-Category Analysis

### Best Performers

- Phones generated the highest sales revenue.
- Copiers generated the highest profit.
- Accessories and Binders showed strong profitability.

### Underperformers

- Tables generated significant losses despite high sales.
- Bookcases generated negative profit.
- Supplies generated negative profit.

---

## Discount Analysis

- Furniture received the highest average discount (17%).
- Furniture generated the lowest profit among major categories.
- Technology maintained strong profitability with lower average discounts.
- Excessive discounting may be reducing Furniture profitability.

---

## Recommendations

1. Increase focus on Technology products.
2. Promote high-profit products such as Copiers and Phones.
3. Review pricing and discount strategies for Furniture.
4. Investigate losses from Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies.
5. Improve profitability in the Central region by analyzing costs and discount practices.
